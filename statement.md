1. Problem Statement
The Student Grade Calculator is designed to simplify the process of calculating a student's total marks, percentage, grade, and pass/fail result. Manually performing these calculations can take time and may lead to calculation errors. The project provides a simple Python-based solution that takes marks for five subjects and generates a basic report card.

2. Scope of the Project
The project focuses on calculating academic results for a student based on marks entered for English, Hindi, Maths, Science, and Computer. It calculates the total marks out of 500, determines the percentage, checks whether the student has passed or failed based on subject marks, and displays the student's grade and result. 


The current project is a simple console-based application and does not include database storage, graphical interfaces, login systems, or multiple-student record management.

3. Target Users
Students – to quickly calculate their percentage and grade.

Teachers – for basic result calculation and verification.

Parents – to understand a student's calculated academic result.

Beginners learning Python – as a simple example of input, arithmetic calculations, conditional statements, and formatted output.

4. High-Level Features
Accepts the student's name.

Accepts marks for five subjects: English, Hindi, Maths, Science, and Computer. 


Calculates total marks out of 500. 


Calculates the student's percentage. 


Checks whether the student passes or fails based on subject marks.

Assigns a grade based on the calculated percentage. 


Generates a simple report card showing the student's name, individual marks, total, percentage, grade, and result.
